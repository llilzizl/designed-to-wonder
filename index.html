01 // The idea

Tech is changing everything. Let’s talk about it.

Designed to Wonder is a hub for tech news, honest answers and real conversations about where we are heading. I write about the launches worth watching, the people who shaped the tools we use, and how to turn clever tech into a better journey for real people.

I’m Lilian: a finance student, web developer and hairstylist. That mix means I care about two things at once, how technology works and how it feels to the person using it.
